SoluTiles
=========

    Paint one landscape. Every edge case covered.
    No pixel you didn't paint.


WHAT THIS IS
------------

An autotile tileset is not hard because of the drawing. It is hard because you do not
know WHAT to draw — and you find out three months later, when one corner of your map has
no tile for it.

SoluTiles gives you a landscape to paint over. Paint it, and you have a complete autotile
tileset: proven complete, not promised. Nothing is mirrored, composited, or generated by
a machine. Every pixel is yours.


GETTING STARTED — FIVE MINUTES
------------------------------

1. Run SoluTiles.exe. There is nothing to install and nothing to configure.

2. In the left bar, under WHAT YOU ARE MAKING, pick your landscape and your pixel size.
   ⚠ These two close as soon as you place your first pixel: changing them later would
   throw the work away, because they would no longer be the same tiles.

3. Paint. Left click paints, right click erases. Eight tools, one key each:
   P B A G L R I E. The brackets [ and ] change the size.

   Cells carrying the same tile fill TOGETHER — it is not another tile that looks alike,
   it is the same one, seen in several places. One stroke can touch a hundred cells.

4. Watch the two gauges at the top right. They say what is left, and they recompute at
   every stroke.

5. When nothing is missing, EXPORT opens. Not before: a half-painted sheet is not a
   tileset, it is a trap.

For the rest, the manual lives inside the software: click the banner at the top. Three
levels — getting started, how it works, and the placement rule itself.


USING IT IN GODOT
-----------------

1. In SoluTiles, left bar, Engine section: "Install the Godot plugin".
2. Point at your PROJECT ROOT — the folder holding project.godot. Not addons/.
3. In Godot: Project -> Project Settings -> Plugins, tick SoluPaint.
4. A SoluPaint button appears in the top bar. Click it: it places a world in the open
   scene and takes you there.
5. Pick a level, arm the brush, and paint your relief. Left click raises, right click
   removes the cell from the world.

A tileset ships with the plugin: yours if it is complete, the trial one otherwise. The
trial is made of the guide's own drawing — enough to place a world right away, without
having painted anything.


REQUIREMENTS
------------

  Windows 10 or later, 64-bit.
  Godot 4.x, for the plugin only. SoluTiles itself needs nothing.

The first time you run it, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". That is
SmartScreen telling you the file is not signed by a certificate authority — not that
anything is wrong with it. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".


WHAT IT DOES NOT DO — YET
-------------------------

Today SoluTiles builds TERRAIN: surfaces, cliffs, multi-level falls, overlapping massifs.

Not in it yet: water, holes, caves, surface materials, slopes, animation. Those are being
built. No dates are promised, on purpose — we would rather say nothing than hold badly.

Updates are included. The early access price only goes up.
